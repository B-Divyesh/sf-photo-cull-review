Terms of use.

Photo Cull Review helps you prepare a human-reviewed file-move plan. It never moves or deletes originals. You remain responsible for checking any exported manifest and for actions you take with a file manager or another tool.

Similarity is a suggestion

SHA-256 matches indicate byte-for-byte identity. Visual hashes are approximate and can group different photographs. Never treat a “likely burst” label as proof that a file is safe to remove. Keep backups and inspect every candidate.

Archive pass

The free product scans folders with up to 750 supported files. The Archive pass is a one-time US$19 purchase that unlocks unlimited scans for the licensed user. Core safety behavior and exports remain free. Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record and handles checkout and refunds. A refund revokes the license automatically.
