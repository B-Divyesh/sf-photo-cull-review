Privacy, in plain language.

Photo Cull Review keeps your media in your browser. It does not upload photos, videos, thumbnails, paths, hashes, decisions, or manifests.

What stays on your device

The app stores thumbnails, hashes, metadata, candidate groups, and decisions in your browser’s IndexedDB. A license token stays in localStorage if you provide one.

Choose New scan to clear the saved workspace. You can also remove site data through your browser.

What leaves your device

Free review never contacts another origin. If you provide a license token, verification is the only cross-origin runtime request.

Buying an Archive pass opens Sociobot/Dodo checkout. Sociobot/Dodo handles checkout and billing as merchant of record.

The app includes no ads, analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or runtime scripts.

Your exports

CSV manifests and JSON workspace backups download locally. They can contain file paths and hashes, so store them carefully.

Contact

Questions may be sent through sociobot.in. This policy changes only when the product’s data behavior changes.
