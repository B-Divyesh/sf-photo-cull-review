Privacy, in plain language.

Photo Cull Review is designed so your media stays yours. It works inside your browser and does not upload photos, videos, thumbnails, file names, hashes, review decisions, or manifests to us.

What stays on your device

The app stores small thumbnails, cryptographic and visual hashes, file metadata, candidate groups, and your decisions in your browser’s IndexedDB. A license token, if you provide one, is stored in localStorage. You can clear the workspace from “New scan” or remove all site data through your browser.

What leaves your device

If you buy or verify an Archive pass, the browser contacts the Sociobot billing API with the license token. Sociobot/Dodo processes checkout and associated billing details under its own merchant-of-record obligations. The app includes no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or runtime scripts.

Your exports

CSV manifests and JSON workspace backups are created as local downloads. You decide where to store or share them. They can contain file paths and hashes, so treat them as private.

Contact

Questions may be sent through sociobot.in. This policy changes only when the product’s data behavior changes.
