How your photo review stays private

Photo Cull Review keeps your media in your browser. It does not upload photos, videos, thumbnails, paths, hashes, decisions, or move plans.

What stays on your device

The app stores thumbnails, hashes, file details, candidate groups, and decisions in your browser. A license token stays in browser storage if you provide one.

Choose New scan to clear the saved review workspace. You can also remove site data through your browser.

What leaves your device

Free review never contacts another website or service. If you provide a license token, verification is the only request sent outside this app.

Buying an Archive pass opens Sociobot/Dodo checkout. Sociobot/Dodo handles checkout and billing as merchant of record.

The app includes no ads, analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or runtime scripts.

Your exports

CSV move plans and JSON workspace backups download locally. They can contain file paths and hashes, so store them carefully.

Contact

Questions may be sent through sociobot.in. This policy changes only when the product’s data behavior changes.
